Vanta TOPOLOGY static artwork

The decorative image assets/vanta-topology-static.webp is a static browser rendering of Vanta.js TOPOLOGY0.5.24 by Teng Bao, based on the topology effect by Kjetil Midtgarden Golid.

Vanta source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/vanta@0.5.24/dist/vanta.topology.min.js
Original effect: https://github.com/kgolid/p5ycho/blob/master/topology/sketch.js
Palette: #A8461F on #FCFCFB. Captured directly from the684x550 canvas after a bounded4.5-second rendering interval, without text overlays. No image repainting or custom noise implementation.

The renderer used p5.js1.9.0 (LGPL-2.1) during asset creation. p5.js and Vanta runtime code are not distributed with this portfolio; the page serves a static WebP. The original canvas pixels were encoded through native browser WebP at quality0.88, without repainting or resizing.
